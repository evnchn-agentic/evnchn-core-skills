---
name: explain-with-image
description: Use when a finding, bug or design needs to be shown as one image for an MR, issue or chat message.
---

1. **Verify first.** Reproduce it and capture the evidence before drawing anything. Nothing unverified goes in the image.
2. **Build what was asked.** One 16:9 image. If it is unclear what should go in it, ask, offering pieces that fit the finding, e.g. a sequence diagram, before/after screenshots, a timeline, a table of measurements, or the quoted error, log line or header.
3. **Lead with the thesis.** The title is the finding in one sentence. Everything else backs it up.
4. **Make it readable from across the room.** Look at the rendered image yourself, fix anything too small or unclear, then render again.
